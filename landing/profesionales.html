How it works

From upload to cleared, tracked the whole way

You see the same pipeline your employer's team sees — every stage has a name, and you always know which one your file is in.

Step 1

Upload your documents

Passport, degree, transcripts, and any prior licence — through a portal branded to the organisation handling your case.

Step 2

Verifier Agent checks them

Every document is checked against its source before anything is submitted anywhere on your behalf.

Step 3

Regulator Agent files and tracks

Submitted to your destination regulator, with status you can check rather than a case number you email about.
